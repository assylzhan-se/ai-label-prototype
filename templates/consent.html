Profile impressions study

This is a student research project at Astana IT University. You will see six fictional dating-app profiles in a research prototype and answer short questions about your impressions. Some images may be synthetic. It takes about 10 minutes.

  • Participation is voluntary and has no effect on grades. You can stop at any time.
  • We do not collect your name, e-mail, photos or account data. Responses are stored under a random study code.
  • Results are reported only in aggregate. You can ask for your data to be deleted using your study code.

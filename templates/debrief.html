Thank you — what this study was about

Participants were randomly assigned to one of two versions of the prototype. In one version each photo had the label “AI-generated image”; in the other there was no label. Everything else was identical. We compare whether such a label changes how genuine and trustworthy a profile seems.

The first six profile photos were generated by AI. The last two photos were real photographs of models from the free stock site Unsplash (photographers Vitaly Gariev and Joseph Sharp); if you saw the "AI-generated image" label on them, that label was not true. We used them to compare how the label works on real and synthetic photos. All names and profile texts were fictional, and a label like this says nothing about the honesty of a real person.

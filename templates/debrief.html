Thank you — what this study was about

Participants were randomly assigned to one of two versions of the prototype. In one version each photo had the label “AI-generated image”; in the other there was no label. Everything else was identical. We compare whether such a label changes how genuine and trustworthy a profile seems.

All profile images were synthetic and all people were fictional. A label like this says nothing about the honesty of a real person.
